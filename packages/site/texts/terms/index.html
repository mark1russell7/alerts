The terms of the alert texts

These terms are for the text messages (SMS) of the alerts program.

These terms apply from 2026-11-17.

The program

  • Program name: alerts.
  • What it sends: alerts about severe weather, power outages, earthquakes and other hazards near the places that a person watches. It also sends tests of the alert path and a message when a test fails.
  • Who gets the texts: only a person who agreed to get them. The opt-in page tells how a person agrees.
  • No marketing: the program sends no marketing and no advertising.

How often

Message frequency varies. The number of texts changes with the hazards. A quiet month has few texts or none. A severe storm can send some texts in one day.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. The program does not charge for the texts.

STOP and HELP

  • Reply STOP to cancel. You get one reply that tells of the stop, and then no more texts.
  • Reply START to get the texts again.
  • Reply HELP for help. The reply gives the name of the program and the address of its support page.

Limits

  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • The texts are not a replacement for the official warnings. Obey the official warnings of your area.

Support and privacy

  • The support page is the issues page of the repository. The issues are public. Do not write a phone number in an issue.
  • The privacy policy tells the data that the program keeps, and what it never shares.
