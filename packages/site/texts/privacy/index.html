The privacy policy of the alert texts

This policy is for the text messages (SMS) of the alerts program. The program sends alerts about hazards to a small group of people. Each person agreed to get the texts.

This policy applies from 2026-11-17.

The data that the program keeps

  • The mobile phone number of each person who agreed to get the texts.
  • The record of each agreement: the date and the time, the way (in writing or in speech) and the version of the consent words.
  • The date and the time of each STOP reply.
  • The time and the delivery status of each text that the program sent.

How the program uses the data

  • The program uses a phone number only to send the alerts of the program, and the answers to STOP and HELP.
  • The program sends no marketing and no advertising.
  • The program keeps the data in a private file on the computers that operate it. The data is not in the public repository of the program.
  • The program sends each text through Twilio, the provider of the texts. Twilio gets the phone number and the text only to deliver the text.

What the program never shares

  • The program never sells, rents or trades a phone number.
  • The program does not share the phone numbers or the consent data with a third party for marketing.

The policy in the standard words of the carriers:

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How to stop the texts and remove your data

  • Reply STOP to a text. The program removes your number from its list, and it sends you no more texts.
  • To remove the other records of your number, tell the operator who asked for your agreement.

Help and contact

  • Reply HELP to a text to get the name of the program and the address of its support page.
  • The support page is the issues page of the repository. The issues are public. Do not write a phone number in an issue.
  • The terms of the texts tell what the program sends and how often. The opt-in page tells how a person agrees.
