How a person agrees to the alert texts

The alerts program sends text messages (SMS) only to a person who agreed to get them. The program has no public sign-up form. The operator of the program adds each person after a clear yes.

The steps of an agreement

  1. The operator gives the person the consent words below, in writing or in speech.
  2. The person answers yes, in writing or in speech.
  3. The operator records the yes on the private opt-in list of the program: the phone number, the date and the time, the way of the yes and the version of the consent words.
  4. The program sends texts only to a number on the list. For each other number, the program sends nothing and records an error.
  5. A STOP reply removes the number from the list at once. A START reply puts it back.

The consent words

Version texts-consent/1

Yes, send me alert texts from the alerts program at this number. The texts tell of severe weather, power outages, other hazards near the places that I watch, and tests of the alert path. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

A sample record

Each yes is one record on the private opt-in list. The list is not in the public repository. This sample has made-up data: its phone number is not a real number.

{
  "number": "+15550100123",
  "at": "2026-11-17T21:30:00Z",
  "way": "written",
  "words": "texts-consent/1"
}
  • number: the mobile phone number, in the international form.
  • at: the date and the time of the yes, in UTC.
  • way: written for a yes in writing, or spoken for a yes in speech.
  • words: the version of the consent words that the person agreed to.

More

  • The terms of the texts tell what the program sends, how often, and STOP and HELP.
  • The privacy policy tells the data that the program keeps, and what it never shares.
  • The support page is the issues page of the repository. The issues are public. Do not write a phone number in an issue.
