# The fixtures of the quake package

- `dyfi-atten-us70005dj8.json` is the real attenuation plot of the DYFI product of the M5.4 event `us70005dj8` in Tanzania (depth 25 km). It draws the two laws of Atkinson and Wald (2007).
- `dyfi-atten-us7000ki5u.json` is the real attenuation plot of the DYFI product of the M5.5 event `us7000ki5u` in Turkey (depth 13.116 km). It draws the two laws of Atkinson, Worden and Wald (2014).
- Each plot gives the MMI at each hypocentral distance in kilometers, to 4 decimals.
- The USGS data is in the U.S. public domain. Credit: U.S. Geological Survey.
